How They Became Part of My Family

Each of my cats has a unique story of how they came to be part of my family. I got all of them from the humane society here in Missoula. Honey was my very first cat and she is the oldest, I adopted her when she was 2 years old. Then a few months later I adopted Maple when she was just a kitten. Then finally one year ago I adopted Bennie with my boyfriend we got him when he was 3 years old, he is now 4 years old.
