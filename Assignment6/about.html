Why I Love Cats

I love my three cats, Honey, Maple, and Bennie, because they are each completely different and have their own unique personalities. I rescued them from the Humane Society here in Missoula, and each of them had a life before they found me. They have stories and experiences that I may never fully know, but I often wonder what their lives were like and how those experiences shaped who they are today. Getting to know them and watching them become comfortable in their home has made our bond even more meaningful.

Honey, Maple, and Bennie each show love in their own way, and they bring something special to my life. They make me laugh, comfort me when I’m having a difficult day, and make my home feel warm and complete. They are much more than pets to me, they are my family, and I love them so much. I’m grateful that we found one another and that I can give them the safe, caring home they deserve. In return, they have brought more happiness, companionship, and love into my life than I could have imagined.
