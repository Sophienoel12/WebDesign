Meet My 3 Cats

A Webpage All About My Furry Friends

Welcome! This page is dedicated to my three cats. Each one has a unique personality, and I hope you enjoy getting to know them. Scroll down to meet Honey, Maple, and Bennie!

Honey

Honey is the oldest. She loves naps and will do anything for pets. Her favorite spot is by the window, soaking up the sun.

Maple

Maple is our mischievous explorer—always climbing, knocking things over, and making us laugh. She’s the most adventurous of the bunch.

Bennie

Bennie is the only boy cat and definitely the cuddliest. He purrs like an engine and loves curling up in your lap whenever he can.
